# Admitting a satellite photograph

1. Start with supplied, licensed originals. Otherwise inspect the **individual** NASA media, Wikimedia Commons, ESA or SpaceX Flickr asset page. Search by mission name, confirm spacecraft, camera viewpoint and image kind. Agency credit alone does not establish rights; check third-party credits and any restrictions. Prefer an in-orbit photograph over hardware on the ground, an official render, then an artist’s concept. Never use generated images or search-engine thumbnails.
2. Record author, exact licence/version, file-page URL, attribution and changes (including crop/re-encoding) in `LICENSE_AUDIT.md`. Retain required licence notices; comply with share-alike where applicable. If uncertain record **needs human decision**, leave the asset out of the manifest. Obtain Persian agency credit and alt text from a native reviewer.
3. Export the approved original at no more than 1200 px wide, WebP or AVIF, at most 150,000 bytes. For ISS, Tiangong, Hubble or Starlink bundle under `app/src/main/assets/satellites/media/`. Use the `file:///android_asset/satellites/media/` URI plus its actual filename. For other approved craft use its verified HTTPS URI indexed by its catalogue NORAD key. Never use a class shape as a family-wide photo key. Starlink is the deliberate exception and has at most one shared image.
4. In `sat_media_manifest.json` change the craft status from `TODO` to `VERIFIED` only after the audit. Each of at most six images requires: `uri`, `kind` (`PHOTO`, `RENDER`, `ARTIST_CONCEPT`), localized agency `credit`/`creditFa`, `author`, `license`, `sourceUrl`, `focal` ([x,y] fractions in original unmirrored pixels), `placeholderColor` (#RRGGBB), `altEn`, `altFa`, `verified: true`. Leave `author` empty only if the credited agency is the sole author. Do not publish guessed sample values.
5. Run `python3 tools/satellites/audit.py` (Pillow with the relevant codec is required to inspect an admitted bundled image) and the Roborazzi review suite described in `review/README.md`. The audit checks recorded fields and files, **not** the truth of a rights claim. When the project owner permits a later device check, inspect airplane mode with cold/warm cache, source links, captions, focal crop, pager in both directions and full-screen zoom/dismiss. If no approved images remain, keep the honest silhouette plate.
