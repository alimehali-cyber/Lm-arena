## Quad-count fit and EXTRAPOLATION to target sizes (labelled extrapolated)

Fit: quads(N) = K * C(N,4) with K = quads/C(N,4) measured at each N:
  N=300: K=4.574e-4 | N=400: K=4.235e-4 | N=500: K=4.486e-4 | N=600: K=4.459e-4
K is stable within +/-4% (finite-N spread), so quads(N) ~ K*C(N,4) holds; using K=4.459e-4
(the N=600 anchor; the 300-600 average would change results by <2%):
  N=9,110 : C(N,4)=2.874e14 -> quads ~ 1.28e11 (~14.0 million quads/star)
            serialized ~ 1.28e11 * 79.93 B ~ 10.2 TB   [EXTRAPOLATED]
  N=15,000: C(N,4)=2.108e15 -> quads ~ 9.40e11 (~62.7 million quads/star)
            serialized ~ 9.40e11 * 79.93 B ~ 75.2 TB   [EXTRAPOLATED]
Build-time extrapolation (O(N^4) loop, measured 15.4 s at N=600):
  N=9,110 ~ 9.5 days single-threaded; N=15,000 ~ 70 days  [EXTRAPOLATED]

## Verdict on the Task-4 "N * 19600/4" quad model (4,900 quads/star, 44.1M @ 9k)

- Bytes-per-quad model (84 B): CLOSE to reality (measured 79.93 B).
- Quad COUNT: catastrophically OPTIMISTIC. The real builder produces ~14 MILLION
  quads/star at 9k scale (measured trend), ~2,900x more than the model's 4,900/star.
  Reason: the model assumed a "nearby limited" cap that DOES NOT EXIST in the code -
  QuadPatternIndex.kt enumerates ALL C(N,4) combinations with only the 40-degree
  separation filter, and CatalogBuildConfig.MAX_STARS_PER_REGION_FOR_QUADS (50) is
  declared but has NO consumers (verified by grep).
- Consequently the pass-1 "3.79 GB / 6.69 GB" figures (which were ESTIMATOR OUTPUT under
  this model, not measurements - correctly relabelled this pass) are ~2,700x too small:
  serialized reality at 9k is ~10 TB (extrapolated from measured 300-600 points).
  The original "10-30 MB" claim was off by ~300,000x.

## Bottom line (what is actually MEASURED vs extrapolated)

- MEASURED: pair index at 9,110 stars = 4,851,922 pairs, 7.5 s build, stars+pairs
  serialize to 78,094,272 B = 74.5 MiB. At 15,000: 13,156,786 pairs, 18.8 s, 211,277,486 B
  = 201 MiB. Quad builder at 300-600 stars: counts above, ~80 B/quad, O(N^4) build times,
  OOM at 800 stars in a 2.8 GB heap.
- EXTRAPOLATED: at 9,110 stars the full quad index would be ~1.28e11 quads / ~10 TB
  serialized / ~9.5 days build; at 15,000 ~9.4e11 / ~75 TB / ~70 days. Infeasible in both
  time and memory AS IMPLEMENTED: the builder needs a per-region star cap (the config
  constant exists but is unused) or pruned neighbor enumeration, and the representation
  needs a fundamentally smaller encoding, before a 9k catalog can ship.
- The pair-only index (74.5 MiB @ 9k, MEASURED) is by itself a shippable size.
