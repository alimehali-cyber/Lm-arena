Glass palette
Aurora: a coral, peach, cyan and violet mesh drifting through a violet night.
What is being shown. The material is four layers: a translucent sheet, the palette's own light re-painted inside the panel's outline, a diagonal specular sweep with weight at the bottom, and a gradient rim (bright where the light is, faint opposite) with an inner bevel. No layer samples the screen behind it — the app has no backdrop capture by design, and the ambient world is soft enough that re-lighting it reads exactly like blurring it.
Glass fillwhite 9 % (Aurora) / rose-white 10 % (Wine), 22–25 px blur behind it
Rim1 px gradient stroke, white .72 → .12 → .40; 2.2 px inner bevel fading from the top
Glowdrop shadow tinted by the palette (violet for Aurora, burgundy for Wine)
